Our story

A neighbourhood café that takes its time.

Harbour Bean started as a coffee cart outside the cannery docks. Today we're a twenty-seat café with the same idea: source beans from small farms, roast them in tiny batches, and serve every cup like it's for a regular.

Our pastry case is filled each morning by our in-house baker, and on weekends the kitchen turns out brunch plates built around Fraser Valley produce.
